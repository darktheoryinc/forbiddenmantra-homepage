# Homepage curation and language-aware homepage

* **No sales data exists**, so nothing is called a bestseller or top-seller. The shelves are titled **Featured books** and say they are not a sales ranking.
* **Own publications:** a dedicated "From Forbidden Mantra Press" section (up to 8 of our titles) sits above the category shelves.
* **Featured books:** up to 4 per subject hub. Score = owner-curated home list (`data/collections.json` `featured.home`) + appearances in collections and reading paths + extra verified editions + has an author page + hand-verified provenance; one book per category when alternatives exist; distinct authors within a shelf. Override any hub with `data/featured.json` (`{"hubs": {"<hub-id>": ["<work id or slug>", ...]}}`). A hub with no third-party book shows our own titles.
* **Nothing is removed:** `data/catalogue.json` is untouched; every book keeps its page and stays reachable through search, category/subject/tradition pages, authors, series and the book-language filter.
* **Language dropdown:** choosing a website language now also (a) states in the menu how many verified editions exist in that language and (b) on the homepage shows a "Books in {language}" band listing only works that have a verified edition in that language, each linking to that edition (`#lang-xx`). If none exist the band says so and shows no books. An English book is never assumed to exist in another language. Today only English has verified editions, so every other language shows the "none verified yet" message until editions are imported. The band's data comes from `window.FM_LANGBOOKS`, generated at build time from the catalogue.
* New interface strings (`lang_home_*`, `lang_status_*`) were added to all eight locales; the non-English wording is machine-assisted and should get native review like the rest of the beta locales.
