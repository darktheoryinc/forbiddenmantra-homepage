Buckland’s Complete Book of Witchcraft

by Raymond Buckland

BeginnerWiccan

A self-study course in modern Wicca, laid out as a series of lessons. Buckland was an early figure in bringing Gardnerian Wicca to the United States, and the book reflects that initiatory-religion perspective rather than historical witchcraft.
