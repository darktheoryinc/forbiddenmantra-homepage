Seventy-Eight Degrees of Wisdom

A Tarot Journey to Self-Awareness

by Rachel Pollack

IntermediateWestern Esoteric

A card-by-card study of the Rider–Waite–Smith tarot that treats the deck as a language of symbols for reflection. It has remained in print since 1980 and is widely used as a foundation text for serious tarot readers.
