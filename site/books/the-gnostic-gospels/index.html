The Gnostic Gospels

by Elaine Pagels

IntermediateGnostic

A scholarly but readable study of the Nag Hammadi texts and what they reveal about the diversity of early Christianity. Pagels examines how the texts differ from the writings that became orthodox and why those differences mattered.

Historical note: Won the National Book Award and the National Book Critics Circle Award. Per publisher and bookseller listings; not a current ranking.
