Energy & Inner Cultivation

Books on cultivating inner energy, mainly Chinese qigong and neigong traditions and the Indian subtle-body models of chakras and kundalini. These are different systems with different vocabularies. Practice claims are reported as the authors present them.

Departments

Taoism

Philosophical Taoism (Laozi, Zhuangzi), religious Taoism and internal alchemy.

6 books · 5 in review

Qigong

Chinese energy-cultivation practices combining movement, breath and attention.

Neigong

Neigong (nei kung) means 'internal work': training the body's inner structure and energy, often in martial or Taoist-influenced contexts.

9 books · 2 in review

Mo Pai

Mo Pai is a neigong lineage presented to English readers mainly through the Mo Pai Nei Kung books.
