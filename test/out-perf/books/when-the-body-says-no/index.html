When the Body Says No

Exploring the Stress-Disease Connection

by Gabor Maté

IntermediateSecular / Clinical

A physician’s argument that chronic suppression of emotion and long-term stress are linked to illness. It is a clinical-psychological work built on case histories, and it is not a substitute for medical treatment.
